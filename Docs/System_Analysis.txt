# System Analysis

## Introduction

System analysis is the process of studying the existing system, identifying its limitations, and proposing a better solution. For this project, the existing inventory and warehouse management process was analyzed to understand the challenges faced while managing cleaning and hygiene products.

## Existing System

Many organizations and warehouses still manage inventory using spreadsheets, manual records, phone calls, and paper-based documentation. Stock details are updated manually, which increases the chances of human error and data inconsistency.

Warehouse staff often spend a significant amount of time checking stock availability, tracking product movement, and maintaining records of incoming and outgoing products.

## Problems in the Existing System

The following problems were identified in the existing system:

1. Manual inventory tracking can lead to incorrect stock records.
2. Difficulty in monitoring product expiry dates.
3. Delays in updating stock information.
4. Lack of centralized warehouse data.
5. Increased chances of stock shortages and overstocking.
6. Time-consuming report generation process.
7. Difficulty in tracking product batches and stock movement.

## Proposed System

The proposed Cleaning & Hygiene Product Warehouse Management System will provide a centralized platform for managing warehouse operations and inventory records.

The system will allow users to:

* Manage product information
* Track stock levels
* Monitor product batches
* Track expiry dates
* Record inward and outward stock movement
* Manage warehouse inventory
* Generate reports
* Receive low stock alerts

The system will be developed using React.js for the frontend, Node.js and Express.js for the backend, and PostgreSQL for database management.

## Benefits of the Proposed System

1. Improved inventory accuracy.
2. Faster stock tracking and management.
3. Better monitoring of expiry dates.
4. Reduced manual work.
5. Efficient warehouse operations.
6. Easy report generation.
7. Better decision-making through organized data.

## Conclusion

The proposed system aims to overcome the limitations of the existing manual inventory management process by providing an efficient, centralized, and user-friendly warehouse management solution. It will help organizations manage cleaning and hygiene products more effectively while improving operational efficiency and inventory control.
