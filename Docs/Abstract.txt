The Cleaning & Hygiene Product Warehouse Management System is a web-based application developed for Ganga Maxx Marketplace to manage inventory efficiently.

The system tracks cleaning products based on category, brand, pack size, warehouse location, batch number, and expiry date. It supports stock entry, stock movement tracking, low-stock alerts, and inventory reporting.

The main goal is to reduce manual effort, improve accuracy, prevent expiry losses, and ensure smooth warehouse operations.
