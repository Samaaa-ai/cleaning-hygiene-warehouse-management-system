# Literature Survey

Before starting the development of the Cleaning & Hygiene Product Warehouse Management System, I studied some existing inventory and warehouse management systems to understand their features and working process. This helped me identify useful functionalities and understand the requirements of my project.

## 1. SAP Warehouse Management

SAP Warehouse Management is widely used in large industries and organizations. It helps in managing inventory, tracking stock movement, and handling warehouse operations efficiently. The system provides real-time inventory updates and detailed reports.

### Advantages

* Real-time inventory tracking
* Supports multiple warehouses
* Detailed reporting and analytics

### Limitations

* Expensive to implement
* Difficult for small businesses to use
* Requires professional training

---

## 2. Zoho Inventory

Zoho Inventory is a cloud-based inventory management software mainly used by small and medium-sized businesses. It helps in stock management, order tracking, and warehouse management.

### Advantages

* Easy to use
* User-friendly interface
* Accessible from anywhere through the cloud

### Limitations

* Limited customization options
* Some advanced warehouse features are not available
* Requires subscription plans

---

## 3. Odoo Inventory

Odoo Inventory is an open-source inventory management system. It provides features such as stock tracking, warehouse management, and inventory control. Since it is open-source, it can be customized according to business requirements.

### Advantages

* Open-source and customizable
* Supports warehouse operations
* Cost-effective solution

### Limitations

* Requires technical knowledge for customization
* Configuration can be time-consuming

---

## Comparative Study

After studying these systems, I observed that they provide common features such as inventory tracking, warehouse management, and stock monitoring. However, they do not specifically focus on cleaning and hygiene product management.

My proposed system will include features such as:

* Product inventory management
* Batch tracking
* Expiry date monitoring
* Stock movement tracking
* Low stock alerts
* Warehouse management
* Compliance and safety record management

## Conclusion

From the literature survey, I understood the importance of inventory management systems in maintaining stock accuracy and warehouse efficiency. The proposed Cleaning & Hygiene Product Warehouse Management System aims to provide a simple and effective solution specifically for cleaning and hygiene product inventory management.
